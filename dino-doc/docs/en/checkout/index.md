# Checkout

The Checkout page is where you finalize your payment for Dino services, such as upgrading your subscription plan or purchasing additional features. This page uses a secure, integrated payment system to process your transaction.

## What You See

When you navigate to the Checkout page, you will see a secure payment form managed by Dino’s payment partner. The page typically includes:

- A summary of the item or subscription plan you are purchasing.
- Fields to enter your payment information (e.g., card number, expiration date, CVC).
- A **Pay** or **Subscribe** button to complete the purchase.
- A **data chat** widget (in the lower corner) — you can use this to ask questions or get real-time help during checkout.

!!! tip "Secure Payment"
    Your payment details are processed securely by our payment partner and are not stored directly within Dino. You will receive a confirmation email once the transaction is complete.

## How to Complete a Purchase

1. **Review your purchase details** at the top of the checkout form.
2. **Enter your payment information** into the secure fields.
3. (Optional) **Use the data chat** if you need assistance or have a question about your order.
4. **Click the "Pay" or "Subscribe" button** to authorize the transaction.

## After Checkout

Once your payment is successfully processed:

- Your account is immediately updated with the new service or subscription level.
- You can return to the main [Dashboard](../dashboard/index.md) to begin using the new features.
- You can view and manage your subscription from your account settings.

!!! warning "Failed Payments"
    If your payment fails, verify your card details and ensure sufficient funds are available. Your account access will not change until a successful payment is completed. If problems persist, use the data chat to contact support.
