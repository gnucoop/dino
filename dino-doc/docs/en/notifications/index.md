# Notifications

The Notifications page shows all messages sent to you by the system or your administrator. From here you can browse, search, and open individual notifications, including ones that link directly to a relevant area of the application.

![Main view of the Notifications page](../imgs/notifications/index.png)

---

## Reading Your Notifications

The list displays every notification you have received, showing its **message text** and the **date it was created**.

A small link icon next to the message text indicates that the notification will take you somewhere when clicked.

The total number of notifications matching your current search is shown at the top of the page.

---

## Searching and Filtering

Use the filter bar above the list to narrow down what is shown.

### Search by keyword

1. Click inside the **search by keyword** field at the top of the list.
2. Start typing any word or phrase from the notification text.
3. The list updates automatically as you type.
4. To clear the search, click the **×** icon that appears inside the field.

### Filter by date

1. Click **Filters** in the toolbar.
2. Pick a **From date** and a **To date** from the calendars.
3. Click **Search**: the list shows only notifications created within that date range. **Reset filters** clears them.

Applied filters appear as chips below the filter bar. Click the **×** on a chip to remove that filter.

---

## Navigating the List

If there are more notifications than fit on one page, use the **pagination controls** at the top of the list to move between pages. You can jump to the first or last page, or step forward and backward one page at a time.

The number of notifications found for your current search is displayed next to the pagination controls.

---

## Opening a Notification

If the notification contains a **link** (shown by the link icon next to the message), click its row: Dino marks the notification as read and takes you directly to the relevant page in the application.

A notification without a link has nothing more to open: its message is the text shown in the list.

---

## Customising the Columns

You can change which columns are visible in the list.

1. Click the **Columns** button above the table, on the right.
2. A panel opens showing all available columns.
3. Toggle the columns you want to show or hide.
4. Close the panel when done — your selection is applied immediately.

!!! tip "Marking as read"
    When you click a notification that has a link, it is automatically marked as read. Notifications without a link are not marked as read from this page.

!!! warning "Opening a link leaves the page"
    Following a notification link navigates you away from the Notifications page to the linked area of Dino. Use your browser's back button to return to the list.
